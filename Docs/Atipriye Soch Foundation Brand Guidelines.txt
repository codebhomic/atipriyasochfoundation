Atipriye Soch Foundation Brand Guidelines
Oct 5, 2026 · @Dr. Ati Priye

These guidelines make every poster, report, post and certificate look like it came from one
organisation. Anyone producing material for the foundation, whether staff, intern, volunteer,
printer or agency, must follow them.

1. Brand essence
Atipriye Soch Foundation exists to change lives by changing thinking. "Soch" means thought:
we believe education, dignity and support begin with how a community thinks about the people
it has left behind.
Who we are: a not-for-profit Section 8 company in Ludhiana, Punjab, working across India.

2. Voice and language

We write the way a respected local teacher speaks: clear, warm and specific. Our sensitive pillars
need extra care with words, because careless language can hurt the people we serve.
How we write
•
•
•
•
•

Short sentences and plain words; avoid jargon and acronyms without explanation.
Specifics over adjectives: "42 children enrolled" beats "many children helped".
People first: they are the heroes of our stories, not the foundation.
Hope over pity: show strength and progress, never helplessness.
Name the language: English for funders and government, Hindi and Punjabi for
communities. Translate meaning, not word for word.

7. Imagery and safeguarding
Our photos show real people with dignity and agency. Safeguarding rules come before any
communication goal.
Photo style

•
•
•
•
•

Real moments in natural light: classrooms, workshops, group sessions, community
events.
People active and engaged (learning, speaking, leading), not posed as recipients.
Eye level, warm tones, uncluttered backgrounds; no heavy filters.
Never use images that show distress, poverty or tears to provoke sympathy.
Stock photos only when no real photo exists; prefer Indian settings and people.
