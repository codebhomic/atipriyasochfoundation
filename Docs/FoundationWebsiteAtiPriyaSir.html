Discussion Summary

Project: Ati Priye Soch Foundation — Website Redesign & Development
Discussion Date: 3 October 2026

1. Project Overview

A discussion was held with Ati Priya Sir regarding the redesign and development of a complete website for the Ati Priye Soch Foundation.

The current website was found to be monotonous and does not properly represent the foundation's vision, activities, and objectives. The goal is to create a professional, engaging, informative, and scalable website that communicates the foundation's work effectively.

2. Reference Websites

Ati Priya Sir shared the following websites as design and content references:

  1. Khalsa Aid — https://khalsaaid.org/
  2. Smile Foundation — https://www.smilefoundationindia.org/
  3. Initiators of Change — https://initiatorsofchange.com/

These will be used for inspiration regarding website structure, visual presentation, and communication style.

3. Information & Documents Shared

Ati Priya Sir has provided the following documents for understanding the foundation:

  • Memorandum of Association (MOA)
  • Articles of Association (AOA)
  • First Activity Report
  • Foundation Bible

These documents will help in understanding the foundation's objectives, structure, and activities and will be used as references while preparing the website content.

4. Key Requirements

  • Complete redesign of the existing website.
  • Clear presentation of the foundation's mission, vision, objectives, pillars, and activities.
  • Modern and professional UI/UX that better represents the foundation.
  • Editable website content so that authorized users can update information without depending on a developer.
  • Proper presentation of programmes, activities, achievements, and future initiatives.
  • Website structure that can support future growth and expansion.

5. Long-Term Vision

Ati Priya Sir discussed the foundation's future expansion, including:

  • Increasing the number of volunteers.
  • Creating different chapters of the foundation.
  • Providing identification/IDs to volunteers associated with different chapters.
  • Expanding programmes through different mediums and locations.
  • Developing an international-level presence.
  • Pursuing opportunities for recognition or listing with organizations such as the United Nations and WHO.

The website should therefore be designed with future scalability in mind rather than only focusing on the current requirements.

6. Next Steps

  1. Study the MOA, AOA, and Activity Report.
  2. Audit the existing website and identify design, content, and technical issues.
  3. Analyze the provided reference websites.
  4. Finalize the sitemap and website content requirements.
  5. Define the required features and future scalability requirements.
  6. Prepare the initial UI/UX design.
  7. Finalize the technical approach and development plan.
