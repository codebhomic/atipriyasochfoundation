Who we are
Atipriye Soch Foundation is a not-for-profit Section 8 company in Ludhiana, Punjab, incorporated on 27 March 2024. Since 29 June 2026 its registered objects formally cover four pillars: child education, women empowerment, men's mental health, and transgender equality.
