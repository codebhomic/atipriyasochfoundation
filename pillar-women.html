Women Empowerment

Transforming lives through market-aligned vocational skills, self-help group microfinance, shelter security, and legal literacy for women.

Sovereignty & Dignity

From Vulnerability to Financial Autonomy

Empowerment is not a charitable gift—it is the restoration of inherent agency, constitutional rights, and financial self-sufficiency.

Anchored in MOA Objects 2 and 3, Atipriye Soch Foundation works with women in informal settlements and peri-urban hubs across Punjab. We replace paternalistic aid with actionable economic assets: certified garment crafting, digital bookkeeping, artisan collectives, and credit linkages.

When a woman earns her own income and understands her legal rights, household health metrics rise, child education is safeguarded, and generational cycles of poverty dissolve.

Program Architecture

Four Pathways to Independence

Holistic programs addressing economic capacity, health dignity, and legal protection.

Vocational Sewing & Crafts

Certified cutting, stitching, and embroidery academies in Ludhiana providing industrial-grade equipment and direct buy-back contracts with textile markets.

MOA OBJECT 2

SHGs & Micro-Finance

Forming Self-Help Groups (SHGs) with peer savings, financial literacy, zero-cost digital bank accounts, and micro-loans for home-based enterprises.

MOA OBJECT 2 & 3

Safe Shelter & Hostels

Emergency temporary housing, short-stay shelter, and residential hostels for deserted women, survivors of abuse, and destitute orphan girls under compassionate care.

MOA OBJECT 3

Legal & Health Literacy

Workshops on domestic violence laws, succession rights, POSH awareness, reproductive health camps, and free distribution of eco-friendly menstrual hygiene products.

MOA OBJECT 3
Statutory Mandate

MOA Foundation & Compliance

Understanding the corporate and legal backing under Table A of Companies Act 2013.

MOA Object 2: Inclusive Skills & Job Training +
Authorizes organizing and imparting specialized employment skills, technical vocational courses, handicrafts, computer proficiencies, and livelihood generation activities targeted specifically at underprivileged women.
MOA Object 3: Equality, Housing & Relief Homes +
Authorizes provision of housing, food, clothing, health, and dignity for vulnerable sections of society; setting up homes, working women hostels, day care crèches, and emergency crisis intervention centres.

Invest in a Woman's Self-Reliance

A grant of ₹2,500 sponsors complete vocational sewing certification and startup toolkit for an aspiring woman entrepreneur.
