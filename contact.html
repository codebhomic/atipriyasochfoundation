Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding statutory registrations, donations, and volunteer operations.

Is Atipriye Soch Foundation a registered non-profit? +
Yes. Atipriye Soch Foundation is a registered Section 8 company under the Companies Act 2013 (CIN: U85500PB2024NPL061137) and is enlisted on the NITI Aayog NGO Darpan portal under unique ID PB/2024/0407003.
Are donations eligible for tax exemption? +
Yes, donations to our registered charitable objects qualify for statutory tax benefits under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. Donors receive an official e-receipt and 10BE form upon reconciliation.
How can I reach the mental health counselling desk? +
For immediate crisis assistance, you can call the nationwide 24x7 toll-free helpline TeleMANAS at 14416. For local peer support circle schedules in Ludhiana and Punjab, select 'Mental Health Support' in the contact form or email our counselling desk.
How do chapter volunteers receive their ID cards? +
After registering on our Get Involved portal and completing our volunteer induction workshop and basic background verification, volunteers are issued an official, barcoded ID badge from our Ludhiana central registry.
