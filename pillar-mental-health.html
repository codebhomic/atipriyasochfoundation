Destigmatizing Vulnerability

Silence Is Not Strength

In India, men account for over 70% of all recorded deaths by suicide. Yet cultural conditioning forces men to suppress pain under harmful stereotypes of "manning up".

On 30 April 2026, by unanimous special resolution, Atipriye Soch Foundation amended its Memorandum of Association to add Object 8. We are one of the pioneering Section 8 institutions in Punjab with an explicit, legally mandated focus on men's emotional wellbeing and crisis intervention.

Working within the statutory framework of the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 and national guidelines set by NIMHANS, we build safe spaces where men and young boys can seek support without shame, fear, or judgment.

Clinical & Community Interventions

Support Systems for Men & Boys

Combining professional psychological care with community-based brotherhood and peer empathy.

Peer Support Circles

Weekly structured, psychologist-moderated support circles across Ludhiana and regional chapters where men discuss work burnout, financial stress, loss, and anxiety safely.

CONFIDENTIAL & ANONYMOUS

First-Aider Training

Training college students, workplace team leads, and community volunteers to spot early warning signs of depression and suicidal ideation, providing timely de-escalation.

EVIDENCE-BASED CURRICULUM

Tele-Counselling

Direct referral and active collaboration with national TeleMANAS nodes and empanelled clinical psychologists to deliver free, telephonic psycho-social therapy.

24x7 ACCESS

Research & De-Stigmatization

Publishing empirical white papers on male agrarian distress, youth unemployment stress, and cultural barriers to therapy in Punjab and northern India.

MOA OBJECT 8
Statutory Compliance under Mental Healthcare Act 2017: Our counselling workflows maintain zero-breach confidentiality protocols, voluntary consent, and structured escalation pathways for clinical psychiatry emergencies.

Our Responsible Communication Code

  • Zero Sensationalism: We never describe methods, means, or specific locations of suicide.
  • Mandatory Help Lines: Every article, post, and workshop prominently features TeleMANAS 14416 and iCall 9152987821.
  • Human-First Language: We use "died by suicide" rather than "committed suicide", and "person living with depression" to eliminate stigma.
  • Safe Storytelling: Community members' personal stories are only ever shared with informed written consent and rigorous anonymization.
