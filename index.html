Our Four Pillars

Where We Work

Every programme we run traces back to a registered object in our MOA. We do not scatter — we go deep, in four carefully chosen areas of change.

Child Education

Child Education

Schools, pre-schools, vocational centres, hostels and scholarships. We run curriculum R&D and ensure student and staff health and welfare.

Learn More →
Women Empowerment

Women Empowerment

Skills and livelihood programmes, safe homes and hostels, and leadership opportunities for women. We recognise agency, not helplessness.

Learn More →
Men's Mental Health

Men's Mental Health

Counselling, peer support, helplines and crisis intervention. We work with NIMHANS and State Mental Health Authorities to break the stigma.

Learn More →
Transgender Equality

Transgender Equality

Education, livelihood, Garima Greh shelter, legal aid and healthcare for transgender persons. We implement the SMILE scheme as an authorised agency.

Our Story

Why We Exist

Founded on 27 March 2024 by Dr. Ati Priye and co-director Rekha Priya, Atipriye Soch Foundation is a registered Section 8 company headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab. "Soch" means thought — we believe change begins with how a community thinks.

  • NGO Darpan Registered: PB/2024/0407003
  • Four pillars formally registered since June 2026
  • Area of operation: Whole of India and internationally

Our Promise

EDUCATE · EMPOWER · INCLUDE

Three words that map precisely to our work: educate the children who have been left out, empower the women and men who have been pushed to the margins, and include every person whose identity has been erased.

EDUCATE

Schools, pre-schools, vocational centres, curriculum and scholarships for children from low-income families.

EMPOWER

Skills, livelihoods and leadership for women. Mental health support and peer networks for men and boys.

INCLUDE

Education, shelter, legal aid and healthcare for transgender, intersex and gender non-conforming persons.
