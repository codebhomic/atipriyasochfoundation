Who We Are

Changing lives by changing thinking. A registered Section 8 not-for-profit foundation based in Ludhiana, Punjab, committed to structural social transformation.

Brand Essence

Rooted In Community, Guided By Evidence

"Soch" means thought. We believe that true change begins with how society thinks about the people it has historically left behind.

Incorporated on 27 March 2024 under the Indian Companies Act, Atipriye Soch Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab. We work across India on four vital pillars: Child Education, Women Empowerment, Men's Mental Health, and Transgender Equality.

Unlike superficial charity models, our work addresses root causes. We combine legal safeguards, evidence-based psychological support, vocational empowerment, and high-quality inclusive education to build self-sustaining dignity for every individual.

Our Core Principles

  • Thoughtful: We lead with empirical evidence, structured research, and field audits, never hollow slogans.
  • Warm & Empathetic: We speak and serve as neighbours, never as condescending saviours.
  • Audited & Compliant: Fully registered under Section 8, NITI Aayog NGO Darpan, and audited annually by independent chartered accountants.
  • Inclusive Without Exception: Breaking societal taboos around men's vulnerability and transgender rights.
  • Rooted & National: Proudly rooted in Punjab's ethos of selfless seva, scaling nationwide across India.

Statutory Mandate

The 9 Main Objects in Our MOA

Every activity, rupee spent, and initiative operated by our foundation is formally authorized by Clause 3(a) of our Memorandum of Association.

MOA Object 1

Educational Institutions

Set up and run schools, colleges, and vocational institutions; collaborate with government bodies and peer NGOs; conduct educational research and specialized curriculum development.

MOA Object 2

Inclusive Education & Skills

Special education and market-relevant employment skills for children, women, senior citizens, and differently-abled individuals; structured sustainable livelihood incubation.

MOA Object 3

Livelihoods & Social Equality

Shelter, food, healthcare, and educational rehabilitation for underprivileged citizens; women's empowerment hubs, residential homes for vulnerable women and orphans.

MOA Object 4

Animal & Nature Welfare

Animal rights, protection of endangered wildlife, environmental preservation, afforestation campaigns, and ecological biodiversity awareness.

MOA Object 5

Disaster Relief Operations

Rapid emergency response and relief for human and animal populations during floods, epidemics, fires, and natural disasters, in tandem with district authorities.

MOA Object 6

School Community Welfare

Medical clinics, pediatric check-up camps, and mental wellness welfare schemes for students, marginalized school teachers, and support staff.

MOA Object 7

Residential & Co-Curricular

Student hostels for youth from remote rural areas; sports coaching, cultural arts development, and holistic leadership building camps.

MOA Object 8 (2026 Addition)

Men's Mental Health & Suicide Prevention

24x7 crisis counselling, peer support, psychological first-aid training, and research on male suicide prevention in accordance with the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 & NIMHANS.

MOA Object 9 (2026 Addition)

Transgender Rights & Inclusion

Advancing rights under Transgender Persons Act 2019; Garima Greh shelter homes; implementation of the Ministry's SMILE scheme; legal documentation, health equity, and livelihoods.
