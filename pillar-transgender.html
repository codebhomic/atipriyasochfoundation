Transgender Equality

Securing constitutional dignity, legal recognition, safe shelter, healthcare equity, and mainstream employment for transgender and gender non-conforming persons.

Constitutional Dignity

Inclusion That Restores Respect

No person should face ostracization, homelessness, or denial of basic civil rights because of who they are. Dignity is non-negotiable.

By unanimous resolution on 30 April 2026, Atipriye Soch Foundation officially incorporated Object 9 into its registered objects. This milestone formally authorizes our foundation to operate as an implementing partner under the Government of India's SMILE scheme (Support for Marginalized Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise) and to run Garima Greh shelter homes.

Under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019, we facilitate official gender certificates, Aadhaar / PAN identity updates, affirmative corporate employment drives, and barrier-free medical healthcare.

DIGNIFIED LIVELIHOODS

Our Affirmative Language & Rights Charter

In line with Section 2 of our Brand Guidelines, Atipriye Soch Foundation insists on language that honours human rights:

  • Respect Chosen Names: We always address persons by their chosen name and self-identified pronouns.
  • Non-Derogatory Terms: We strictly avoid pejorative labels, using "transgender person", "trans woman", or "trans man".
  • Zero Forced Outing: Information on identity is kept strictly confidential unless disclosed voluntarily by the individual.
